In this note we discuss $I=\frac{1}{2},I_3=+\frac{\pi}{2}$ pion nucleon 
scattering in lattice qcd. We first construct the interpolating
for the $\pi N$ interacting states using the Clebsch-Gordan coefficients:
(constructed using the isospin 1 pion and the isospin 1/2 nucleon)
\begin{equation}
\vert \pi N, I=\frac{1}{2},I_{3}=\frac{1}{2}\rangle=+\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\vert 1,+1\rangle\vert \frac{1}{2} -\frac{1}{2}\rangle
                                                   -\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}\vert 1, 0\rangle\vert \frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\rangle
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\vert \pi N, I=\frac{1}{2},I_{3}=\frac{1}{2}\rangle=+\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\pi_+ n
                                                   -\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}\pi_0 p
\end{equation}
We use the following interpolating operators for mesons and baryons:
\begin{eqnarray}
\pi_+=\bar{d}\Gamma_M u \\
\pi_+^{\dagger}=\bar{u}\Gamma_M d\\
\pi_0=\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\bar{u}\Gamma_M u-\bar{d}\Gamma_M d\right)\\
\pi_0^{\dagger}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\bar{u}\Gamma_M u-\bar{d}\Gamma_M d\right)\\
p=N_{+}=(u^t\Gamma_N d)u \\
p^{\dagger}=N_{+}^{\dagger}=(\bar{d}^t\Gamma_N\bar{u})\bar{u}\\
n=N_{-}=(d^t\Gamma_N u)d \\
n^{\dagger}=N_{-}^{\dagger}=(\bar{u}^t\Gamma_N\bar{d})\bar{d}\\
\end{eqnarray}
Summarizing we have the following interpolating operator
for $piN$ state in the $I=\frac{1}{2},I_3=\frac{1}{2}$ channel:
\begin{eqnarray}
\vert\pi N,I=\frac{1}{2},I_3=\frac{1}{2}\rangle =
\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}(dud)\times(\bar{d}u)-\sqrt{\frac{1}{6}}(udu)\times(\bar{u}u)+
\sqrt{\frac{1}{6}}(udu)\times(\bar{d}d)\\
\langle \pi N,I=\frac{1}{2},I_3=\frac{1}{2}\vert =
\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}(\bar{u}\bar{d}\bar{d})\times(\bar{u}d)-\sqrt{\frac{1}{6}}(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u})\times(\bar{u}u)+
\sqrt{\frac{1}{6}}(\bar{d}\bar{u}u)\times(\bar{d}d)
\end{eqnarray}
Thus we have to calculate the following contractions:
\begin{eqnarray}
       \frac{2}{3}(dud)-\bar{d}u \times \bar{u}d-(\bar{u}\bar{d}\bar{d}) \\
\sqrt{\frac{1}{9}}(dud)-\bar{d}u \times \bar{u}u-(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u}) \\
\sqrt{\frac{1}{9}}(dud)-\bar{d}u \times \bar{d}d-(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u}) \\
\sqrt{\frac{1}{9}}(udu)-\bar{u}u \times \bar{u}d-(\bar{u}\bar{d}\bar{d}) \\
       \frac{1}{6}(udu)-\bar{u}u \times \bar{u}u-(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u}) \\
       \frac{1}{6}(udu)-\bar{u}u \times \bar{d}d-(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u}) \\
\sqrt{\frac{1}{9}}(udu)-\bar{d}d \times \bar{u}d-(\bar{u}\bar{d}\bar{d}) \\
       \frac{1}{6}(udu)-\bar{d}d \times \bar{u}u-(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u}) \\
       \frac{1}{6}(udu)-\bar{d}d \times \bar{d}d-(\bar{d}\bar{u}\bar{u}) 
\end{eqnarray}
In the following sections we look all the diagrams occur in the above 
contractions. In order to compute the diagrams we estimate the sink to
sink propagators stochastically. In the stochastic inversion we only invert
for the UP quark and we are using 12 stochastic random vector with full-time
dilution. Including the spin half and three-half case we have two
different possibilities for the sink to sink propagator:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U\left(x_{f1},x_{f2}\right)$: We have the following stochastic part in the reductions
\begin{itemize}
\item ${\mathcal V}_3(\xi, \Gamma_{f2}P)$, where $P$ is a point or sequential source propagator, and $\xi$ 
is the stochastic source
\item ${\mathcal V}_2(\phi, \Gamma_{f1}P_1,P_2)$, where $P_1,P_2$ are point or sequential source propagators, and $\phi$
is the stochastic $UP$ propagator computed from $xi$.
\end{itemize}
\item $D\left(x_{f1},x_{f2}\right)$: We have the following stochastic part in the reductions
\begin{itemize}
\item ${\mathcal V}_3(\gamma_5\phi, \Gamma_{f2}P)$
\item ${\mathcal V}_2(\gamma_5\xi, \Gamma_{f1}P_1,P_2)$
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
